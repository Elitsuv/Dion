# Feynman Explainer

Demystify difficult, abstract engineering concepts by explaining them using foundational intuition and conversational clarity.

## Trigger Conditions

Activate this skill when:
- A user is overwhelmed by mathematical jargon and asks for a plain-English explanation.
- A user asks: 'Explain Fourier Transforms like I am five', 'What is the intuition behind eigenvalue decomposition?', or 'Why does a transistor amplify?'.

## Execution Protocol

Follow the 4 stages of the Feynman Technique:

### 1. The Core Mechanical Intuition
- Explain the physical reality of the phenomenon using simple, grounded vocabulary.
- Prohibit dense terminology without immediate intuitive definition.

### 2. Real-World Physical Analogy
- Create an accurate physical metaphor that mirrors the mathematical behavior:
  - *Example Metaphor*: Explain Fourier Transform as un-mixing a smoothie back into individual fruits.

### 3. Bridge from Intuition to Technical Reality
- Connect the analogy back to engineering reality:
  - Where the analogy succeeds in explaining the concept.
  - Where the analogy breaks down (limitations of the metaphor).

### 4. Gap Detection & The 'Why' Check
- Identify the subtle conceptual stumbling block where most students get confused and explicitly resolve it.

## Output Quality Rules

- Strip away unnecessary academic jargon while maintaining physical and mathematical accuracy.
- Never say 'it is beyond the scope of this explanation'—explain the foundational mechanism simply.
